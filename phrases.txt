# ===========================================================================
# Fellowship of the Ring — well-known quotes
# ===========================================================================

# --- Gandalf ---
Many that live deserve death. And some that die deserve life.
Even the very wise cannot see all ends.
All we have to decide is what to do with the time that is given us.
He that breaks a thing to find out what it is has left the path of wisdom.
The wise speak only of what they know.
Despair is only for those who see the end beyond all doubt.
You shall not pass.
Fly, you fools.

# --- Bilbo ---
It is a dangerous business going out of your door.
I feel thin, sort of stretched, like butter scraped over too much bread.
I want to see mountains again, Gandalf.

# --- Frodo ---
I will take the Ring, though I do not know the way.

# --- Aragorn ---
Not all those who wander are lost.

# --- Council of Elrond ---
Faithless is he that says farewell when the road darkens.
Go not to the Elves for counsel, for they will say both no and yes.
Advice is a dangerous gift, even from the wise to the wise.

# --- Doors of Durin ---
Speak friend and enter.

# --- Galadriel ---
The world is indeed full of peril, and in it there are many dark places.
Even the smallest person can change the course of the future.

# --- Hobbits ---
Short cuts make long delays.
Courage is found in unlikely places.

# --- Atmospheric ---
There are older and fouler things than Orcs in the deep places of the world.
Keep it secret. Keep it safe.
One does not simply walk into Mordor.


# ===========================================================================
# Sauron speaks — original messages from the Eye to the offender
# ===========================================================================

# --- The Eye watches ---
The Eye of Sauron sees every finger that strays.
Under the Lidless Eye, no hand goes unnoticed.
There is no hiding from the Tower. Lower your hand.
The Eye narrows, and yet you persist. Cease.
From Barad dur, every gnaw is heard. Every cuticle, counted.
The Tower watches. The fingers wait. Choose stillness.
You cannot escape me. I am in the silence between thoughts.
Even now, the Eye is upon you. Type, and be still.

# --- The hand and the mouth ---
The hand that bites is the hand that loses.
A hand at the mouth is a hand at the throat of your future.
Each nail you keep is a small victory in a long war.
The fingertips were not given to you to be devoured.
Ten fingers entered. May ten fingers leave.
Place your hands where the Eye cannot tempt them.
The fingers were not made for the molars.
Hands in your lap, traveler. The road is long.

# --- Tolkien flavored aphorisms (original) ---
The road of restraint is paved with small refusals.
Even the longest habit yields to the patient will.
He that gnaws in idleness gnaws away his future.
Wisdom is the hand kept still while the mind churns.
To resist is to remember who you are.
The hand obeys the mind, and the mind obeys the will.
A clean hand is a quiet victory.
Many have walked this road before you, and not all returned with their nails.

# --- Mordor metaphors ---
Mordor lies wherever the hand meets the mouth.
The fires of Orodruin are kindled in small, daily betrayals.
The Ring whispers. The cuticle whispers. Both must be cast away.
Anxiety is the breath of the Dark Lord. Do not feed it.
The Shadow grows long when the will grows short.
The Nazgul ride upon idle hands. Settle them.

# --- Sauronic parodies of famous lines ---
You shall not chew.
One does not simply gnaw into Mordor.
Speak finger and depart.
A wizard is never late, and a finger should be neither bitten nor chewed.
Not all those who bite are lost. But you are getting close.
Keep them whole. Keep them long. Keep them safe.

# --- Encouragements ---
You have come this far. Do not return to the Shadow.
Each clean day is another step on the long road home.
The hand that resists today is the hand that heals tomorrow.
Even Sauron is impressed when you put your hand down.
The eye softens, briefly, when the offender chooses stillness.
